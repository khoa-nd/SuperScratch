The "Repeat Until" Block

A super fun guide for curious 7-year-old coders.
Let's learn how to tell the computer: "Keep doing this... until something happens!"

1. The Story 2. The Block 3. Watch It Work 4. Compare 5. Real Games 6. Try It 7. Quiz 8. Video Ideas

1 The Cookie Story 🍪

Imagine you have a plate of cookies. Your job is simple:

Eat one cookie...
then eat another cookie...
then eat another cookie...
UNTIL the plate is empty!

You don't know exactly how many cookies are on the plate. Maybe 3. Maybe 8. Maybe 100! 😱

You just keep eating until there are zero cookies left. That's it! That's the same idea as the repeat until block in Scratch.

The rule of "repeat until":
  1. Do something.
  2. Check: did the special thing happen yet?
  3. If NO → do it again.
  4. If YES → stop! Move on. 🎉
Other real-life examples:
  • Climb stairs until you reach the top.
  • Pour water into a cup until it's full.
  • Brush teeth until they are clean.
  • Run around the playground until Mom calls you.

2 Meet the Block 🧩

In Scratch, the repeat until block lives in the orange Control drawer. It looks like a hugging "C" shape that wraps around other blocks like a bear hug! 🥧

This is Scratch Cat. He helps us see the blocks in action.

This is what it looks like:

repeat until  
move 10 steps
 

The little diamond < > shape is where you put a question the computer can answer "yes" or "no".

A real example:

when ⚑ clicked
repeat until touching edge?
move 10 steps
 
say I made it!

Translation: "Walk forward 10 steps over and over... until you bump into the wall. Then say I made it!"

The big idea: "repeat until" needs a question (the diamond) and some blocks inside (the bear hug). The computer keeps doing the inside blocks again and again until the question's answer becomes YES.

4 Three Loop Cousins 👪

Scratch has three loop blocks. They are like cousins, but each one is a little different.

🔁 repeat 10

repeat 10
move 10 steps
 

"Do this exactly 10 times, then stop."

Use when: you know the exact number of times.

∞ forever

forever
move 10 steps

"Do this forever and ever, never stop."

Use when: you never want it to stop, like a heart that always beats.

⏳ repeat until

repeat until at goal?
move 10 steps
 

"Do this until something special happens, then stop."

Use when: you don't know the number of times, but you know when to stop.
The most important difference:
🔁 repeat 10 → you tell it the number.
⏳ repeat until → you tell it the condition (a yes-or-no question).

7 A Tiny Peek at "Real" Code 💻

When you grow up and learn languages like Python or JavaScript, "repeat until" has a different name: it's called a while loop. But the idea is the same!

The Scratch way:

repeat until x > 200
change x by 10
 

The Python way (just for fun!):

# Keep adding 10 to x, until x is bigger than 200
x = 0
while x <= 200:
    x = x + 10
print("I made it!")
See? Same idea, different costume! The computer keeps doing something until a question becomes true.
